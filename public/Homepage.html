Aircon advice

Service, Repair or Replace? A Quick Guide for Adelaide Homes

Straight answers to the questions we hear most, so you know what you're booking before you call.

Signs you need air conditioning repairs

  • Blowing warm or weak air on cooling mode
  • Water dripping from the indoor unit
  • Ice on the copper pipes or the outdoor unit
  • Grinding, rattling or squealing noises
  • Switching on and off every few minutes (short cycling)
  • An error code flashing on the unit or controller
  • Power bills climbing while your usage hasn't changed

Switch the system off if you see ice or water, and call us on 1300 019 568. Running it can make the damage worse.

How often should you service your aircon?

For most homes, once a year, ideally in spring, before the summer rush. Systems that run hard all summer, evaporative coolers and commercial refrigeration benefit from more frequent servicing. Between services, clean split system filters every few weeks during heavy use.

When does replacing make more sense than repairing?

When the system is old and needing repeat repairs, still runs on R22 refrigerant (being phased out in Australia), or can't hold the house at a comfortable temperature on hot days. We'll tell you straight which way we'd go and why, and point you to any rebates currently available.

FAQ

Air Conditioning Questions We Get Asked

How often should I get my air conditioner serviced?

Once a year suits most homes, ideally in spring before Adelaide's summer demand peaks. Systems that run hard all summer, evaporative coolers and commercial refrigeration benefit from more frequent servicing. A service covers filter and coil cleaning, refrigerant checks and a full system diagnostic, which catches small faults before they become breakdowns.

Why is my air conditioner blowing warm air?

Common causes are a blocked filter, the wrong mode or temperature setting, a dirty or iced-up coil, an outdoor unit that has tripped or lost power, or low refrigerant from a leak. Check the filter and settings first. If it still blows warm, switch it off and book a repair, because running a system low on refrigerant can damage the compressor.

Do you service and repair ducted, split system and evaporative air conditioners?

Yes. We install, service and repair ducted reverse cycle, split system and evaporative air conditioning, as well as commercial refrigeration, coolrooms and freezer rooms. If you're not sure what type of system you have, email a photo of the indoor or outdoor unit and we'll identify it.

Should I repair or replace my air conditioner?

Repair usually makes sense when the system is relatively young and the fault is isolated. Replacement is often the better spend when it needs repeated repairs, runs on R22 refrigerant, or can't keep the house comfortable on hot days. We'll inspect it and tell you plainly which option we'd choose and why.
